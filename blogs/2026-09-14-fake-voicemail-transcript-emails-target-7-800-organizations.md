Automated voicemail transcripts have become part of the daily rhythm of enterprise communication. They arrive with familiar subject lines, system-generated formatting, and little human context, exactly the qualities that make them easy to trust and easy to overlook. Attackers are now exploiting that familiarity, turning routine call-transcription notifications into a vehicle for credential phishing.
