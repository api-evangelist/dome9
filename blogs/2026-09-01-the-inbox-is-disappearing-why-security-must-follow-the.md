Enterprise work no longer fits neatly inside a defined perimeter. The modern workspace spans inboxes, browsers, SaaS applications, collaboration platforms, identities, endpoints, data stores and, increasingly, AI agents that can act across several of these environments at once. Attackers have adapted to this reality faster than many enterprise security programs have.
