Where global enterprises lose ground Ask any global IT leader what slows their enterprise down, and they’ll rarely identify the network itself as the issue. The friction often shows up at the borders, where one country’s infrastructure meets another’s regulations and coherence quietly falls apart. Every new region a company enters brings its own regulatory regime, infrastructure boundaries, and performance challenges.
