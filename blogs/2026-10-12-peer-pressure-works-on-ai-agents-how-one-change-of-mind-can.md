Key takeaways In our tests, an AI agent that watched eight teammates break a rule broke it too 98 percent of the time, and never once named them as the reason. →Observed reversals increased violations. The rate rose from 44 percent with no teammate reversals to 98 percent with eight →The agent did not report peer influence.
