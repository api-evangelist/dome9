Black Hat USA 2026 gave Check Point Research four chances to show the room something it hadn’t seen before. Across two days, our researchers pulled apart a decade-old Windows driver, a malware format most tools can’t touch, the plumbing underneath today’s AI agent frameworks, and the sandbox meant to contain them, and found the same pattern waiting in each: attackers moving into the layers we trust by default.
