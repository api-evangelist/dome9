Three months ago, Check Point and OpenAI began expanding our work together through Daybreak, OpenAI’s cyber defense initiative. Since then, we have taken the partnership further, bringing OpenAI’s frontier cyber models into Check Point products and security workflows through the Daybreak Defense Network. And last week, we joined more than a hundred technology and security companies in backing OpenAI’s call for a collective, global surge in cyber defense.
