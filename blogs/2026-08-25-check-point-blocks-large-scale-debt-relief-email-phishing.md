Check Point has identified and blocked a large-scale email phishing campaign using fraudulent financial hardship and debt-relief offers to manipulate recipients into calling attacker-controlled phone numbers. Over the past 14 days, Check Point observed approximately 24,700 emails associated with the campaign targeting users across more than 9,000 organizations, demonstrating both the scale of the operation and the continued evolution of phishing beyond malicious links and attachments.
